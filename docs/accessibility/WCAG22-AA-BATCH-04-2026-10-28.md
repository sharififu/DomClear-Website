## Small scoped change

Change only the foreground utility on these 11 white CTA links, from `!text-[#1a86f0]` to `!text-[#1D4ED8]`. The blue and purple hero gradients, button white surfaces, hover opacity, text, external signup URLs, analytics handling, page content and SEO markup are unchanged.

Normal-state colour contrast against white is calculated as **3.67:1 → 6.70:1** (WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.3 minimum is 4.5:1 for normal-sized text). New `tests/accessibility/blue-cta-contrast.spec.ts` gates actual rendered button counts, computed foreground/background, destinations, and axe colour-contrast in hovered translucent states on seven routes.
