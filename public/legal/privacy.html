Privacy Notice – DomiClear Ltd

Last updated: 11 August 2026

It also explains our role when a care provider uses DomiClear to manage information about its service users and staff.

2. When DomiClear is a controller and when we are a processor

DomiClear is a data controller when we decide why and how personal information is used for our own business activities. This includes activities such as:

  • creating and administering DomiClear customer accounts;
  • managing subscriptions and billing;
  • responding to enquiries, demos and support requests;
  • operating and securing our website and platform;
  • maintaining business, compliance and security records; and
  • communicating with customers and prospective customers.

When a care provider uses DomiClear to store and manage information about service users, relatives, staff or care delivery, the care provider will normally be the data controller and DomiClear will act as its data processor.

In those circumstances, the care provider decides why the care information is processed and provides instructions to DomiClear. The care provider's own privacy notice should explain how it uses that information. DomiClear supports the care provider in meeting its data protection obligations.

5. Customer-controlled care and workforce information

DomiClear is designed to help care providers manage care and operational records. Where a customer uses these features, the customer may upload or create information including:

  • service-user names, contact information and identifiers;
  • care plans and care notes;
  • medication and eMAR records;
  • visit records;
  • incidents and risk assessments;
  • health and social care information;
  • photographs, wound images, documents and attachments;
  • digital signatures;
  • relatives, representatives and emergency-contact details;
  • staff identity, role and work information;
  • audit records; and
  • precise location information related to care visits where the relevant feature is enabled.

Precise location data

Where a care provider enables visit verification, the app records precise GPS coordinates (latitude and longitude), together with the reported accuracy of that reading, at the point a member of staff clocks in and clocks out of a care visit. These arrival and departure coordinates are stored against the visit record and are used to confirm that a visit took place at the expected address.

Precise coordinates are also recorded against service-user addresses where a care provider uses the app to capture an address location.

Location is collected only while the app is in use for these actions. DomiClear does not collect continuous or background location.

Some of this information may be special category personal data, particularly health and care information and, depending on the customer's use of the platform, information such as ethnicity, religion or other protected characteristics.

For this customer-controlled information, DomiClear normally processes the information only on the care provider's documented instructions. The care provider is responsible for identifying its lawful basis and any applicable condition for processing special category information.

7. Why we use personal information and our lawful bases

Where DomiClear acts as a controller, our main purposes and lawful bases are:

Providing accounts and subscriptions

We use account, contact and subscription information to provide DomiClear and administer customer relationships.

Lawful basis: contract, or taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.

Billing and financial administration

We use billing and transaction information to take and record payments, manage subscriptions and maintain appropriate financial records.

Lawful basis: contract and, where applicable, legal obligation.

Customer support and communications

We use contact details and correspondence to respond to enquiries, provide support, arrange demonstrations and manage customer relationships.

Lawful basis: contract where the communication relates to a customer service, and legitimate interests in operating and developing our business where appropriate.

Security, fraud prevention and audit

We process security, authentication, technical and audit information to protect accounts, investigate problems, prevent misuse, maintain service integrity and demonstrate accountability.

Lawful basis: legitimate interests in protecting DomiClear, our customers and users, and legal obligation where a law requires us to keep or disclose information.

Legal, regulatory and compliance activities

We may use information to meet legal obligations, respond to regulators or lawful requests, manage disputes and maintain security and compliance records.

Lawful basis: legal obligation and, where appropriate, legitimate interests in establishing, exercising or defending legal rights and managing our business responsibly.

Marketing

Where we send marketing communications, we will rely on consent where consent is required by law, or legitimate interests where direct marketing is permitted without consent. You can opt out of marketing communications at any time.

9. Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information.

We may share personal information with suppliers and service providers where this is necessary to operate DomiClear. Depending on the processing involved, these may include:

  • Supabase for database, authentication, file storage, serverless functions and backend services;
  • Stripe for subscription billing and payments;
  • Vercel for website/application hosting and the technical request data (such as request URLs, IP address and timestamps) generated when the web app is served;
  • Resend for sending transactional and operational email, such as invitations, account email, notifications and invoices. Resend receives the recipient's email address and the content of the message;
  • Expo (Expo Application Services) for delivering push notifications to mobile devices. Expo receives the device push token and the content of the notification;
  • Google (Google Analytics 4 and Google Ads) for website and web-app analytics and advertising measurement, on the web version only;
  • Microsoft where business records relating to DomiClear are held or synchronised in OneDrive as part of our current business-administration arrangements;
  • Hostinger for business email and domain services;
  • GitHub for private source-code management, where production customer data is not intended to be stored; and
  • other professional advisers, auditors, regulators, law-enforcement bodies or authorities where disclosure is required or permitted by law.

Under our current architecture, care records are requested by the app directly from Supabase and are not routed through Vercel. We do not intentionally place health or other special category care information into Vercel Customer Data. Request URLs handled by Vercel can contain record identifiers, such as a service-user or visit identifier, and those identifiers may appear in hosting logs.

We do not send care records, health information or special category information to Google, Resend or Expo. Notification content sent through Expo and email content sent through Resend is limited to operational messages, which may include a person's name and the nature of the task or alert.

Where we appoint a processor, we require appropriate contractual and security protections. Where DomiClear is itself acting as a processor for a care provider, we only appoint subprocessors in accordance with our contractual obligations to that customer.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, we use an applicable lawful transfer mechanism. Depending on the supplier and destination, this may include:

You can contact us at info@domi-clear.com if you would like more information about the safeguards used for a particular transfer.

11. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including any legal, accounting, security or regulatory requirements.

The criteria we use include:

  • how long an account or customer relationship remains active;
  • how long information is required to provide or support the service;
  • applicable legal, tax, accounting or regulatory obligations;
  • the time reasonably required to investigate security events or resolve disputes; and
  • any contractual instructions that apply where DomiClear acts as a processor.

Where DomiClear processes care-provider data as a processor, retention and deletion are governed by the customer's instructions and our data-processing terms. At the end of the service, information is deleted or returned in accordance with those instructions, subject to applicable legal requirements and agreed backup arrangements.

We are continuing to formalise our detailed records-retention schedule. This notice will be updated if our retention practices materially change.

12. How we protect information

We use technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information. These include, as appropriate:

  • access controls and role-based permissions;
  • multi-factor authentication for privileged cloud accounts;
  • encryption in transit;
  • database access controls and row-level security;
  • logging and audit controls;
  • endpoint firewall and anti-malware protection;
  • restricted administrative access; and
  • security reviews and vulnerability/remediation processes.

No system can guarantee absolute security. We review and improve our controls as DomiClear develops.

Uploaded files and document links

Files uploaded to DomiClear are held in our Supabase storage buckets. Access works in one of two ways depending on the bucket.

Some buckets are private. Files in those buckets can only be retrieved through a time-limited link that the app generates for a signed-in, authorised user and that expires shortly after it is issued.

Other buckets are currently configured for direct link access. A file in one of those buckets is reached through a long, unguessable web address. Anyone who has that address can open the file without signing in, and the address does not expire. You should therefore treat links to uploaded files as confidential and share them only with people who are authorised to see the file.

We are migrating the remaining file types to private buckets with expiring links. This notice will be updated when that work is complete.
