Cookie Policy – DomiClear Ltd

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy covers the DomiClear web app at domiclear.app and our marketing website at www.domi-clear.com. It supplements our Privacy Notice. DOMICLEAR LTD is registered in England and Wales under company number 17383911, with its registered office at 17 School Lane, Chellaston, Derby, DE73 6TF.

1. Essential cookies and storage

We use necessary cookies, browser local storage and device storage to provide sign-in, sessions, account security and the requested care-management features. Disabling essential storage can prevent the app from working.

Your optional tracking preferences are stored locally by DomiClear under the key domiclear_tracking_consent_v1. This contains the consent version, the optional purposes selected and when you made the choice. It is not an advertising identifier.

2. Analytics and advertising on the web app (optional)

Where configured, the DomiClear web app offers two separate optional purposes. Neither is enabled by default:

  • Google Analytics 4 (Analytics): measures selected public signup and entry pages. DomiClear does not deliberately send patient, staff, visit or other care-record page paths as GA4 page-view events.
  • Google Ads (Advertising): supports advertising-conversion measurement on selected public pages.

The app does not request the Google tag script until you give an affirmative choice for a relevant purpose. Rejecting optional tracking does not prevent use of the care features.

Where you allow a Google purpose, Google may receive page, browser, device and approximate-location details and may set identifiers including _ga or _gcl_*, depending on the purpose and Google's operation. We do not deliberately send care records or special-category health information to Google for analytics or advertising.

3. Analytics on the marketing website

The marketing website uses Vercel Web Analytics. Vercel describes its analytics as cookie-free and based on aggregated measurements. This is separate from the web app's optional Google tags. Hosting and security systems may still process technical request information; see the Privacy Notice.

4. How you choose and withdraw

When optional Google tags are configured on the web app, you can select Accept all, Reject all or Customise. Customise lets you independently select Analytics and Advertising, with both options initially off.

The Cookie settings control remains available in the web app so you can change or withdraw your choice. Withdrawal stops new tracking events initiated by DomiClear's own code, updates Google's consent settings and attempts to remove applicable first-party Google cookies.

If a third-party script was already loaded before you withdrew consent, it may remain in the browser's memory until you reload the page. We recommend reloading when convenient, after saving any work in progress. You can also clear cookies through browser settings. Blocking essential storage may affect the app.

5. Further information

For supplier, international-transfer and data-protection information, see our Privacy Notice. Contact info@domi-clear.com with questions.
